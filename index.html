    How a project runs

    Four stages, each with something you can see and use before we move on.

    1. Discover

      We talk through your week and find the tasks that eat the most time for the least value.

      Free call, then a short plan

    2. Prototype

      A working version of one tool, tested on your real questions, documents or data.

      About 2 weeks

    3. Connect

      We plug it into the apps your team already uses, with sign-offs where a person should decide.

      1–3 weeks

    4. Support

      We watch quality, fix what drifts and train your team so the tool keeps earning its place.

      Monthly, cancel any time

    Common questions

    Do we need technical staff to use what you build?

    No. Tools are set up so your team works in apps they already know, and we train them on anything new. We handle hosting, updates and fixes.

    Is our data safe?

    We only connect the data a tool needs, keep access limited to named people, and explain in plain language where data is stored and which providers see it. Sensitive work can use private or local models.

    What if the AI gets something wrong?

    We design for it. Tools show their sources, hand over to a person when unsure, and keep a log so you can review and improve answers.

    Can you improve a tool we already have?

    Yes. We can review an existing chatbot or automation, find what is failing and either repair it or rebuild the weak parts.

    Tell us what slows your team down

    Describe the task in a few lines. We will reply within one working day with questions or a suggested next step.
